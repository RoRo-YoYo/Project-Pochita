# Project-Pochita
Status: In Active Development

This repository hosts the ongoing mechanical design files for an electromechanical animatronic. The primary focus is currently on designing a rigid internal skeleton capable of housing a high-torque DC motor while maintaining the structural integrity of a soft-body textile shell.

## 📐 Current Engineering Focus: CAD & Mechanisms
I am currently iterating on the internal chassis design in **SolidWorks**. Key design challenges being addressed:

* **Design for Assembly (DFA):** Designing the motor mount and switch housing as a multi-part assembly with snap-fits and screw bosses to ensure serviceability.
* **Tolerance Analysis:** Adjusting interference fits (0.1mm - 0.2mm offsets) for 3D printed parts to ensure the DPDT switch is held firmly without needing excessive adhesive.
* **Space Optimization:** Modeling the component layout (Battery Pack, Motor, Switch) to minimize internal volume, ensuring the plushie remains soft and huggable while housing rigid mechanics.
